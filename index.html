HDMapVLM

Vision-Language Model for Prior-Guided HD Map Updating

Overview

High-definition maps provide essential geometric context for autonomous driving, but their contents become stale as road layouts change. HDMapVLM updates an aligned yet potentially outdated prior HD map from seven synchronized surround-view camera observations.

The method formulates map updating as multimodal autoregressive sequence generation. Camera images, a textual task instruction, and a serialized prior vector map form a shared context. The model then generates the complete current map, preserving stable structures, removing obsolete elements, and inserting newly observed dividers, pedestrian crossings, and road boundaries.
